Here's a focused list, one main book per subject wherever possible. With limited study time around a full-time job, it's better for her to read one book well than five partially.

## Screening Test

**Indian History**
- **Ancient & Medieval:** NCERT *Themes in Indian History* Parts I & II (Class 12), plus the old NCERT books by R.S. Sharma (Ancient) and Satish Chandra (Medieval)
- **Modern:** *A Brief History of Modern India* by Rajiv Ahir (Spectrum), which is the standard book

**Geography**
- NCERT Class 11 *Fundamentals of Physical Geography* and *India: Physical Environment*
- *Certificate Physical and Human Geography* by G.C. Leong, for world physical geography
> Telugu Akademi's *Andhra Pradesh Geography*, for the AP part

**Indian Society**
- NCERT Sociology Class 11 and 12 (*Indian Society*, *Social Change and Development in India*)
- *Indian Social Problems* by Ram Ahuja, for social issues
- Welfare schemes: AP Socio-Economic Survey (welfare chapter) and the official AP government scheme websites

**Current Affairs**
- One monthly magazine with an AP focus, e.g. Eenadu Pratibha or Sakshi Education (both have AP coverage)
- One daily newspaper: Eenadu or The Hindu (AP edition)

**Mental Ability**
- *A Modern Approach to Verbal & Non-Verbal Reasoning* by R.S. Aggarwal
- *Quantitative Aptitude for Competitive Examinations* by R.S. Aggarwal, for the numeracy and data analysis chapters only

## Mains Paper I

**Section A: AP History & Culture (75 marks)**
- Telugu Akademi's *History and Culture of Andhra Pradesh*, which is the core book; most toppers use it
- *History of Modern Andhra Pradesh* by P. Raghunadha Rao, for the British period, the Andhra Movement and the formation of the state

**Section B: Indian Constitution (75 marks)**
- *Indian Polity* by M. Laxmikanth, which covers everything from the Preamble to the 73rd/74th Amendments, the commissions and anti-defection
- *Introduction to the Constitution of India* by D.D. Basu, only as a reference if needed

## Mains Paper II

**Section A: Indian & AP Economy (75 marks)**
- *Indian Economy* by Ramesh Singh, or the shorter NCERT Class 11 *Indian Economic Development* to start
- **AP Socio-Economic Survey (latest year).** This is the most important source for AP economy. It is free on the AP Planning Department website.
- Summaries of the latest Union Budget and AP Budget. Her Treasury work gives her a head start here.
- Telugu Akademi's *Andhra Pradesh Economy*, for structured AP content

**Section B: Science & Technology (75 marks)**
- *Science and Technology* by Ravi P. Agrahari (McGraw Hill), or the Spectrum S&T book
- *Environment* by Shankar IAS, for ecology, biodiversity, pollution and climate change
- The Economic Survey chapters on energy and health, for recent schemes and figures

## CPT
No book is needed. She should practise on a computer with MS Word, Excel and PowerPoint, and use a free typing site such as typing.com or Ratatype for 15 minutes a day.

## Most important of all
- **Previous year question papers** from psc.ap.gov.in. Solving them shows exactly what APPSC asks and which topics to skip.

## Tips on the books
- **Medium:** the exam is bilingual. Telugu Akademi books come in both Telugu and English, so she can pick whichever language she reads faster.
- **Editions:** buy the latest edition of the Laxmikanth, Ramesh Singh and S&T books. Older editions miss recent amendments, schemes and data.
- **Budget:** if she has to cut down, the essential five are Laxmikanth, the Telugu Akademi AP History book, the AP Socio-Economic Survey, R.S. Aggarwal Reasoning, and a monthly current affairs magazine.
